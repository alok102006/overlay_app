VideoGate

Pick your apps and a video. The video plays first, and the app opens only after it ends.

Download for Android (APK)

Install

  1. Open the downloaded file. If asked, allow installing from this browser.
  2. Open VideoGate, tap Choose video and pick your video.
  3. Tap Choose apps and select the apps that should play it first.
  4. Tap Turn on accessibility service, find VideoGate and switch it on.

If the accessibility switch is greyed out

  1. Long-press the VideoGate icon, then App info.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu and choose Allow restricted settings.
  3. Go back and turn the service on.

Android only. VideoGate uses the accessibility service just to know which app is opening. It does not read your screen, and your video never leaves your phone.
